In one line: ANPD’s 2027-2028 rule queue left internal planning and became a public consultation with a deadline.

## What happened

On 1 September 2026 ANPD opened a call for input on the 2027-2028 Regulatory Agenda and the 2027-2030 Regulatory Outcome Evaluation Agenda. Contributions go through the Brasil Participativo platform until 16 October.

Announced axes include promoting a safe digital environment for children, adolescents and women — parental supervision, safe platform design, use of AI by minors, transparency and action against illicit content generated by AI tools — plus privacy and personal-data protection. The planned evaluation includes the International Data Transfer Regulation and the Guiding Guide on Age-Assurance Mechanisms.

## Why it matters

After the Digital ECA and ANPD’s transformation into an agency, regulatory planning is no longer only an internal document. Whoever processes a child’s data or operates an AI system needs to know what enters the rule queue with a deadline and a target.

Taking part in the call is a way to anticipate what is coming. Ignoring it is receiving the rule already finished. The cut on children, women and generative AI is not abstract: it is the same axis the authority already used in platform monitoring and in a recent sanction.
