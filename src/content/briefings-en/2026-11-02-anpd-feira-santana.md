**In one line:** official publication of lists with health data without prior control generates an infringement even without a fine.

## What happened

On 26 October 2026 ANPD concluded the administrative sanctioning process against Feira de Santana City Hall. The decision recognized six LGPD infringements for the exposure, in September 2025, of the names of 1,241 people with diagnoses of HIV, sickle cell anemia and fibromyalgia in the Municipal Official Gazette. The city hall removed the publication the same day, but did not notify the agency or the data subjects and did not respond to inspection requests.

As a public body, there was no fine. Sanctions include a warning, publication of the infringement on the site for six months and corrective measures (DPO, policies, response plan, DPIA, flow registration and a prior stage before publication in the Official Gazette).

## Why it matters

Communication of a sensitive-data incident requires a record-by-record inventory. A generic note on the site does not replace the contact required by the Regulation. Without a map of what was exposed and who was reached, the response remains incomplete even when the material is taken down the same day.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |

| Absence of incident communication | ANPD recorded lack of notification to the agency and data subjects; postal attempt only for 22 | High |
| Governance and minimization | Six recognized infringements, including purpose and security measures | Medium |

## What to do this week

1. Review publication flows for official lists that may contain health or sensitive data — DPO / legal.
2. Confirm that the data inventory allows identification of affected data subjects in case of exposure — privacy team.
3. Validate that the incident communication policy provides for individualized contact, not only a public note.
