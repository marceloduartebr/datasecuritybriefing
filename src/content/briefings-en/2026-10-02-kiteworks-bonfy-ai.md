In one line: knowing where the data sits is not enough; the market is buying the decision at the moment it leaves, including when the sender is an agent.

## What happened

Kiteworks announced the acquisition of Bonfy.AI, a content-security startup founded in early 2024 by Gidi Cohen and Danny Kibel that left stealth in June 2025 with a US$9.5 million seed. The price was not disclosed; CTech estimates "tens of millions of dollars," a figure the companies have not confirmed. According to the companies, Bonfy classifies data at the moment of exchange and applies policy before the transfer finishes, with a single model for people and machines across email, file sharing, SaaS, and AI agents. It is Kiteworks' eighth acquisition in five years.

## Why it matters

Inventory and classification at rest are now the baseline. The risk is in the transfer — and AI agents already send email and files on behalf of users, often outside DLP policy.
