In one line: TJMG confirms leak of some files, but judicial and administrative systems remain intact.

## What happened

On 14 November 2026 the TJMG verified the disclosure of some leaked files after a cyber security incident detected in the preceding days. Technical analyses confirm there was no compromise of judicial or administrative systems nor loss of database integrity. IT teams blocked accounts and reset passwords at risk. Potentially impacted data subjects are being contacted individually, under LGPD. ANPD and police were notified.

## Why it matters

The system that supports the delivery of justice stayed up. The file that left the perimeter did not. Without a record-by-record map, individual notification depends on a list that has not yet been published. The interval between detection and public confirmation was short; the interval until the complete inventory is still open.
