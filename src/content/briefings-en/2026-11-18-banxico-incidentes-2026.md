In one line: Banxico counts 13 cyber incidents in the financial system in 2026, triple the previous years.

## What happened

By 30 October 2026, the Bank of Mexico had received reports of 13 cyber incidents from entities in the national financial system. Four hit banks, four hit electronic payment fund institutions (IFPE), three hit popular financial companies (sofipos) and two hit savings and loan cooperatives (socaps). The total exceeds the four cases in each year from 2023 to 2025. A Qilin ransomware case in February caused 91.7 million pesos of impact to the entity, mainly in electronic transfers. In most events customers had no direct loss.

## Why it matters

The regulator's public figure shows frequency, not the victim's name. The recurring vector is still the application that prepares or executes the transfer. Without an inventory of which service accounts and systems sit on that path, the response stays in the consolidated report instead of the preventive control.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Ransomware / transfers | 13 cases reported; one with 91.7 M pesos | High |
| Customer impact | Most with no direct loss reported | Low |
| Concentration | Four banks and four IFPE | Medium |
