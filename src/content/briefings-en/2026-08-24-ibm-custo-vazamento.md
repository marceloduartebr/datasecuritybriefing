**In one line:** the regulatory fine is only a slice of the price.

## What happened

IBM’s Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026, released in July, put the global average cost of a breach at US$ 4.99 million — a series record. In the US, the average rises to US$ 11.5 million. Vector No. 1, for a fourth year running, is still phishing.

## Why it matters

A relevant share of that cost comes from response time: the longer it takes to learn what was exposed, the larger the financial and reputational hit. Teams that price an incident only as a fine ignore downtime, lost customers, litigation and the cost of people putting out the fire.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Record average cost | IBM 2026 | High |
| Phishing as the entry door | IBM, 4th year | High |

## What to do this week

1. Put discovery time into the risk model, not only the sanction amount — owner: risk / CFO + CISO.
2. Tie the phishing exercise to the data map that user can reach.
3. Signal it worked: the next simulated incident has an estimated cost beyond an “ANPD fine”.
