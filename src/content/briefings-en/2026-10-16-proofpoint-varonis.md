In one line: Proofpoint buys Varonis to unite sensitive data mapping with the email and agent channel.

## What happened

On October 15, 2026, Proofpoint announced a definitive agreement to acquire Varonis for approximately $6.3 billion in cash. Shareholders receive $49.75 per share. Closing expected in the first half of 2027.

## Why it matters

The inventory of where data lives and who has access starts feeding the control of what the AI agent can reach. Without it, the approved AI policy remains a declaration.
