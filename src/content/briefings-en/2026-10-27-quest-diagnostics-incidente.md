In one line: Quest laboratory portal had unauthorized access to patient records; system restored, map of data subjects still open.

## What happened

On October 21, 2026, Quest Diagnostics identified unauthorized access to Quanum LSM. Filed 8-K on 10/22 stating that patient records were accessed. On 10/23 (7pm ET) declared the incident remediated, the system restored and available. Confirmed reach of PHI and PII; exact scope remains under investigation. Notified authorities and engaged specialists. Core operations were not materially impacted.

## Why it matters

Order and results portal concentrates clinical data and identification. Fast restoration does not resolve the time between detection and record-by-record map. Notification to data subjects depends on evidence still being assembled.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Access to patient records (PHI/PII) | 8-K and company statement | High |
| Incomplete scope | Company states investigation ongoing | Indeterminate |
| Operational impact | Company states core operations not materially interrupted | Low |

## What to do this week

1. Map laboratory order/results portals and which patient data they store — owner: IT / privacy.
2. Test ability to list, within 48 hours, data subjects and records reached in a portal access — owner: incident response.
3. Confirm that the communication plan to data subjects does not depend on “system restored” without reach evidence.
