In one line: US$150 million to map and control what AI agents touch in data.

## What happened

On November 4, 2026, Sentra announced a US$150 million Series C led by Capital One Ventures. The company reports growth above 300% in the last year and total investment above US$250 million. The platform classifies data at rest and maps human identities, service accounts, and AI agents that can reach them, with analysis in the customer's environment. Capital One takes a board seat.

## Why it matters

AI agents become intensive consumers of data. A policy of permitted tools does not equal evidence of which records and inherited permissions each agent has already accessed. Without an inventory of data + non-human identity, the reach only appears after unexpected behavior.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Agent with inherited access to unclassified data | Round announcement and platform description | High |
| Lack of visibility into non-human identities | 300% growth and focus on agents | Medium |
| Analysis outside the customer environment | Sentra states in-situ analysis | Low |

## What to do this week

1. Inventory active agents and copilots and the permissions they inherit — owner: identity / data security team.
2. Classify the sets these agents can reach and mark the high-risk ones — owner: data governance.
3. Define an alert for unexpected agent access to a sensitive set — signal: ticket or log reviewed within 24h.
