In one line: a notice on the website is not a data-subject notification.

## What happened

On 8 July 2026 ANPD opened a Sanctioning Administrative Proceeding against Instituto Saúde e Cidadania (Isac), a social organisation that runs public health units in Bahia, Goiás, Rio Grande do Sul, Alagoas, Piauí and Tocantins. Case 00261.003381/2026-55 covers a 2025 ransomware attack that, according to the authority, exposed data of about 500 thousand patients — 78,772 children and adolescents and 47,921 older people — including identification and health data (tests, records, prescriptions, attendances, admissions, diagnoses and procedures). ANPD points to insufficient communication: the site notice did not state the incident date, the nature of the data or the measures taken; there was no individual notice to data subjects. Isac claimed no relevant risk or harm without proving it. Defence deadline: ten business days from service. Sanctions under article 52 of the LGPD.

## Why it matters

The attack is not the differentiator. It is the distance between “something happened” and “what happened to each person’s data”. Without a record-by-record inventory the organisation can only issue a generic statement. With a child’s health data, that gap becomes the proceeding itself.

## What to do this week

1. Simulate an individual notice: data subject, data category, system, date — owner: DPO + operations.
2. Separate the institutional statement from the technical proof of what left.
3. Signal it worked: a draft ready for the data subject, not only the banner on the site.
