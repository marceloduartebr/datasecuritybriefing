In one line: ten business days to prove, not to declare.

## What happened

On 21 August 2026 ANPD started monitoring 22 digital agents in Brazil. First group (13): Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp (public channels), Telegram, TikTok, X, Discord, YouTube, Kwai, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Pinterest and Reddit. Second group (9): App Store, Google Play, Copilot, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, ChatGPT, Meta AI and Perplexity. The cut cites the Marco Civil (Decrees 12.975 and 12.976/2026) and the Digital ECA, in force since March. Deadline: ten business days from notice.

## Why it matters

The authority now treats an AI assistant with the same weight as a social network when the topic is third-party content, children and women. That only holds with logs, classification and retention — not with what the terms claim.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
