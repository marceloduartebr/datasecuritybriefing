In one line: ANPD puts the update of sanction dosimetry into public consultation to cover ECA Digital and new Marco Civil rules.

## What happened

On October 15, 2026 the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority opened a public consultation on the draft update of the Regulation on Dosimetry and Application of Administrative Sanctions (Resolution CD/ANPD No. 4/2023). The proposal incorporates competencies linked to the Digital Statute of the Child and Adolescent and to Decrees 12.975 and 12.976. The consultation remains open on the Brasil Participativo platform until November 30, 2026. An online public hearing is scheduled for October 29; registrations for oral statements run until October 24.

## Why it matters

Dosimetry is no longer only about LGPD and now calibrates sanctions for failures in age verification and other child and adolescent protection obligations. Platforms and controllers need to turn written policy into auditable technical evidence.

## Risk reading
