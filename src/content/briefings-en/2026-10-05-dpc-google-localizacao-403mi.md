In one line: Google's lead EU authority closed the location inquiry with €403 million and a six-month compliance order.

## What happened

DPC decision of 21 Sep 2026, own-initiative inquiry opened in February 2020 after complaints (including BEUC). Period: 25 May 2018 to 4 Feb 2020. Features: Web & App Activity, Location History, Location Accuracy. Infringements: lawfulness/fairness (WAA and LH), transparency (all three), retention (WAA and LH). Fine of €403 million; compliance within 6 months. An Irish fine is enforceable only after court confirmation.

## Why it matters

The case is about lawful basis and retention of a location signal — the same data apps and fleets keep in LATAM without a retention map.
