**In one line:** A municipal management software provider suffered ransomware and interrupted operations of about 250 Argentine municipalities.

## What happened

On October 23, 2026, Program Consultores S.A., based in Córdoba and provider of the PGM platform for more than 250 municipalities in 11 provinces, had servers encrypted by ransomware. Tax collection and digital procedure platforms went offline. The company issued a statement denying database leakage, reported a complaint to the prosecutor's office and stated it is working on restoration. Municipalities, including Villa Carlos Paz and Cosquín, reported ransom demands of up to three bitcoins. As of October 27 some localities were still recovering services.

## Why it matters

Concentration of critical infrastructure in a single provider turns a third-party incident into operational disruption for dozens of administrations. The company's denial of leakage does not eliminate the need for a clear inventory of what each municipality processes and evidence that data were preserved. Written vendor policy does not substitute measurable restoration capability.
