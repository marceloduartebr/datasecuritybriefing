In one line: 3corações notified Mercafé customers of unauthorized access to name, CPF, phone and order, and is already seeing phishing in the brand's name.

## What happened

Grupo 3corações' data protection officer, Felipe Holanda Ribeiro, sent individual notices to Mercafé e-commerce customers. The text describes unauthorized access to the store environment, operated by a third-party cloud provider, via anomalous requests between 17 and 25 September 2026. The company says the incident was confined to that environment.

Data that may have been accessed are full name, CPF, mobile phone and order details and status. The notice states that passwords, credit card and other financial information were not exposed. It does not state the number of data subjects nor, in the customer text, record notification to ANPD.

Containment described: deactivation of the compromised access key on 25 September, blocking of malicious URLs and hiring of an intelligence firm for scanning and investigation. The DPO warns of active SMS phishing campaigns simulating brand promotions. Contact: privacidade@3coracoes.com.br. As of 13 October there is no press coverage.

## Why it matters

The immediate risk is the CPF + phone + order pair, not the card. That feeds identity fraud and social engineering, and the company itself already observes SMS in the brand's name. The environment is third-party cloud: useful evidence is the compromised key and the provider's scope, not only the containment narrative.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Access to name, CPF, phone and order | DPO notice | High |
| Password, card, financial data | Company states they were not exposed | Low, per controller |
| Phishing in brand name | DPO reports active SMS campaigns | High |
| Volume of data subjects | Not stated | Indeterminate |
| Notification to ANPD | Not in the customer text | Indeterminate |

## What to do this week

1. Treat SMS and WhatsApp in the name of 3corações as a scam channel, not support.
2. If you are a data subject, ask what exactly was accessed and whether ANPD was notified.
3. Note that the source is the controller's notice, still without independent coverage.
