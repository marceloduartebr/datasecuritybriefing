In one line: SABG is treating a Telegram offer attributed to Aeroméxico as a data incident; the airline is investigating and has not confirmed.

## What happened

On 20 September 2026 the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno warned of possible exposure in a system linked to Aeroméxico. On 18 September a Telegram user offered a 1.10 GB database with more than 15 million records (name, email, phone, mobile, date of birth, enrollment date). The authority obtained a sample of 100,092 records, including servers and public figures, and opened an official investigation under articles 54 and 55 of the LFPDPPP. Aeroméxico said it is investigating authenticity and origin, has not identified financial data, card numbers or passwords so far, and asked customers to be wary of unusual contacts. It has not confirmed that the database belongs to its operations.

## Why it matters

An offer on a criminal channel is not the same as a confirmation by the victim. If the customer file is genuine, notifying data subjects and the authority requires a record-by-record map — not the text of the privacy policy.
