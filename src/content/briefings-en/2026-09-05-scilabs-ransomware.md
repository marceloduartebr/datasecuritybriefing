In one line: volume rises and the map shrinks — fewer countries, more attacks, three brands with almost half the activity.

## What happened

On 4 Sep 2026 El Economista published SCILabs (Scitum) figures on ransomware observed in Latin America in the first half of 2026. There were 290 attacks and 50 variants, against 231 attacks and 47 variants in the second half of 2025 (+25.5%). Affected countries fell from 23 to 21. Brazil 25.17%; Mexico 17.93% (~52 cases); Colombia 11.03%. The Gentlemen 49, Qilin 46, LockBit 5.0 43 (47.59% together). Services 19.66%, government 11.72%, manufacturing 8.28%. The base is telemetry + investigation + response — not a blocked attempt.

## Why it matters

Concentration changes the governance question: it is not enough to know the region got worse. You need to know which process, which third party and which data set would fall into that 47%.
