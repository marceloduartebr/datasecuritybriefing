In one line: ANPD requires proactive action from platforms to prevent harm to children and adolescents, not just incident response.

## What happened

On October 28, 2026, the National Data Protection Agency published the Guidance Guide on the scope and general obligations of the ECA Digital. The text defines the duty of prevention as the obligation to identify, assess, and mitigate risks throughout the product and service lifecycle, before violations occur. It includes dimensions of prevention, protection, information, and security, with examples of privacy-by-default settings, age verification, and content moderation.

## Why it matters

The guide shifts the predominant reactive logic. Technical evidence of mapping and mitigating risks specific to minors becomes the core of compliance, beyond formal policy. Platforms with “probable access” by children and adolescents must demonstrate concrete measures, not just a non-targeting declaration.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Absence of proactive risk mapping for minors | Guide requires identification and mitigation before harm | High |
| Permissive default settings | Privacy by design and maximum protection by default | High |
| Lack of age verification or parental supervision | Cited as protection measures | Medium |

## What to do this week

1. Review whether the company’s products fall under “probable access” by minors — DPO and product.
2. Map specific data and behavior risks for children/adolescents and document mitigations — security and privacy.
3. Check default settings for minor accounts and moderation mechanisms — engineering.
