In one line: a minimum-age policy does not replace technical evidence.

## What happened

On 25 August 2026 ANPD fined ByteDance, TikTok’s controller, R$ 153.7 million — the authority’s largest sanction to date. The Official Gazette decision describes processing of children’s data on the open feed (no account) and on the logged-in feed without an adequate legal basis, and no proof of effective mechanisms against child sign-ups. Among the measures: automatic restrictive settings for under-16s and a halt to advertising on the no-login version.

## Why it matters

Almost every platform declares a minimum age. What ANPD demanded was a demonstration of what the system does with the data — including what enters through the open feed and through integrations. LGPD accountability is not settled in the terms of use.
