In one line: Chilean consular visa system under persistent attack for two weeks; forensic analysis underway and ANCI investigating.

## What happened

El Mostrador published on 11/11/2026 that Chile's Ministry of Foreign Affairs faces a cyberattack on the Consular Attention System (SAC), impacting visa registration. Reported vector: phishing on diplomatic email with self-replicating malware (every 10 minutes). Foreign Ministry confirmed forensic analysis. ANCI confirmed investigation and suspicion of DDoS. More than one million Chileans abroad depend on the system.

## Why it matters

Operational continuity versus containment: shutting down the system would paralyze consular procedures. Reveals lack of isolation and access inventory in state identity infrastructure.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
