**In one line:** the DSPM market is capitalizing agent governance — data plus non-human identity under the same control.

## What happened

On 22 September 2026 Cyera received $400 million from Goldman Sachs Alternatives Growth Equity, an extension of the Series G led in June by Evolution Equity. Valuation remains above $12 billion. Year to date: $400 million (January) + $600 million (June) + this check = $1.4 billion in 2026. In July the company announced the acquisition of Oasis Security (agentic access), reported at $1 billion. Stated use: AI security roadmap, federal market and EMEA/APAC expansion. Journalistic source: SecurityWeek; complementary primary: Goldman Sachs AM statement.

## Why it matters

This is not a valuation post. It is the design capital is paying for: joining discovery of sensitive data with who — or what — has a path to it, including the agent. In LATAM the same design shows up in cloud RFPs, Copilot and LGPD audits — without the Israeli check.
