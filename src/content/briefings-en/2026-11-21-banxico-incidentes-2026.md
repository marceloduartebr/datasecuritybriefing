In one line: Banxico counts 13 cyber incidents in the financial system in 2026, triple the previous years.

## What happened

By October 30, 2026 the Bank of Mexico had received reports of 13 cyber incidents from entities in the national financial system. Four occurred at banks, four at Electronic Payment Fund Institutions (IFPE), three at popular financial companies (sofipos) and two at savings and loan cooperatives (socaps). The total exceeds the four cases in each year from 2023 to 2025. A Qilin ransomware case in February caused 91.7 million pesos of impact to the entity, mainly in electronic transfers. In most events there was no direct loss to customers.

## Why it matters

The regulator's public figure shows frequency, not the name of the victim. The recurring vector remains the application that prepares or executes the transfer. Without an inventory of which service accounts and which systems sit on that path, the response stays in the consolidated report instead of preventive control.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Ransomware / transfers | 13 cases reported; one with 91.7 M pesos | High |
| Impact on customers | Most with no direct loss reported | Low |
| Concentration | Four banks and four IFPE | Medium |
