In one line: ANPD kept the suspension because Discord did not technically prove the block covers every required channel.

## What happened

On 24 September 2026 the ANPD Enforcement Superintendency denied the reconsideration request and kept Discord live streams suspended in Brazil. The appeal goes to the Board of Directors. The agency said the evidence does not show, in a technical and verifiable way, a full Go Live block. The feature has been suspended since 12 August; the inspection has run since 7 August over risk to children and adolescents. A compliance plan and gradual reactivation were refused at this stage.

## Why it matters

An unavailability notice does not replace reproducible evidence. A child's data cannot be swapped later.
