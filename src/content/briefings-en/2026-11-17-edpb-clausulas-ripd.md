In one line: there is a recognised regional contract for the second hop of European data, but it only works with traceability.

## What happened
On 13/11/2026 the European Data Protection Board published a statement adopted at its latest plenary supporting the use of other models of contractual clauses in onward transfers of personal data received under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). The statement welcomes the update to the European Commission’s FAQs on the SCCs, which recognises that the Council of Europe Model Contractual Clauses (CoE MCC) and those of the Ibero-American Data Protection Network (RIPD MCC) may also be used for those transfers, under the conditions described in the FAQs. The EDPB says it worked together with the Commission and that the statement provides practical guidance for anyone wishing to use those clauses. The EDPB page was not re-read for this briefing; the data comes from the reading recorded by the radar.

## Why it matters
The RIPD brings together data protection authorities from Ibero-America. Latin American companies that receive European data and pass it on to partners in the region now have a recognised regional model for that onward transfer.
