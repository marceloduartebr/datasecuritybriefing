**In one line:** the Civil Police of the Federal District took down Mind-7, a platform that sold by subscription queries to Brazilians' personal data from breaches and failures in public systems.

## What happened

On 24/09/2026, PCDF launched Operation Perseu 7 against Mind-7. The platform charged a monthly fee for CPF or CNPJ queries that returned name, parentage, address, documents, links and court cases. According to police, the database held hundreds of millions of records obtained from breaches and from failures in systems of public bodies, professional councils and telecom operators.

Thirteen warrants were served in SP, MG, PI, PR, ES and PE, with more than 80 officers. The court blocked up to R$ 7 million and ordered the platform taken offline. The group moved more than R$ 7 million between March 2021 and March 2026, used a front company, straw men and cryptocurrency, and was preparing a second platform.

## Why it matters

Leaked data does not disappear after the incident: it is aggregated, enriched and resold for years. Mind-7 fed frauds such as the fake-lawyer scam, in which the criminal uses real case data to gain the victim's trust. For companies, that means clients can be approached with true information in their name.

## Risk reading

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Scope | Nationwide, hundreds of millions of records |
| Exposed data | Registry, family, documents, links and court cases |
| Criminal use | Social engineering and fraud (fake lawyer) |
| Origin | Breaches and failures in public systems, councils and operators |
| Residual risk | High: data still circulates and there is indication of a successor platform |

## What to do this week

1. Review integrations that query public or operator databases and apply volume limits, query logging and anomalous-use alerts.
2. Tell clients your company never asks for payment or data by message, and provide an official confirmation channel.
3. Signal it worked: out-of-pattern queries raise an alert within 24 hours and support starts logging scam attempts in your name.
