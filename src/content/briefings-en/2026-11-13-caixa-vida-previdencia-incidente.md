In one line: Caixa Vida e Previdência (investee of Caixa Seguridade) confirmed unauthorized access and copy of registration data from the base, including the 7.6 million active clients; no preliminary indications of financial data or passwords.

## What happened

In a market communication sent to the CVM on November 12, 2026, Caixa Seguridade reported that its investee Caixa Vida e Previdência (CVP) identified, on November 6, unauthorized access and copy of personal and registration data outside its technological environment. ANPD, other authorities and affected data subjects were notified on November 11. Names and CPFs from the registration base were exposed, which includes clients and former clients and covers the totality of the 7.6 million active clients; in part of the records, also addresses, phones, emails and dates of birth. The preliminary investigation did not indicate compromise of financial or authentication data (policy values, pension balances, bank data or passwords). The company states it has no evidence, up to the communication, of public disclosure or improper use. The vector was not disclosed; the mechanisms used were blocked and forensic analysis is underway with external consultancy. ANPD confirmed receiving the communication on 11/11 and said it is verifying the measures, especially transparency and damage mitigation. The incident reached CVP, not the systems of Caixa Econômica Federal as a bank.

## Why it matters

Names and CPFs of 7.6 million pension and insurance clients, in part with phone and email, raise the risk of social engineering and frauds that impersonate CVP itself or other institutions — alert that the company makes in the communication to data subjects.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Copy of registration data (name, CPF) from the complete base | CVM communication, 7.6 million active clients + former clients | High |
| Address, phone, email and birth in part of the records | CVM communication | High |
| Financial data or passwords | No indications in preliminary investigation | Undetermined |
| Access vector | Not disclosed | Undetermined |

## What to do this week

1. Confirm official channels of CVP and Caixa Seguridade and guide clients and teams to distrust contacts that ask for password, token or additional data — owner: customer service / communication.
2. Reinforce phishing and social engineering alert that impersonate pension, insurance or Caixa — owner: security / fraud.
3. Follow developments of the CVM communication and ANPD analysis.
