In one line: Einstein admits unauthorized acquisition of part of its patient database and maintains that the data was not disclosed.

## What happened

On 8 October 2026, Hospital Israelita Albert Einstein said a cyberattack resulted in unauthorized access to and acquisition of part of its patient database. The suspicion was identified on 4 August. The note, reported by Valor, says there is no evidence of disclosure or misuse.

The records are registration data — name, CPF and/or date of birth — and may include health data, such as prescriptions and exam results. The hospital says the material is fragmented and that a relevant share is loose documents, many without a data-subject identifier. The number of subjects was not disclosed. Identified patients are being notified by email. The electronic health record stayed online and care did not stop.

ANPD was notified and the case was taken to the Federal Police. The review includes independent experts.

The Federal Police, in a 7 October note, does not name the victim. Operation Insidioso executed a search warrant and a temporary arrest warrant in Joinville (SC), ordered by the São Paulo Regional Guarantees Court. The investigation describes ransomware, extraction of sensitive data and extortion; the suspect allegedly facilitated the extraction. The note refers to a report in July. The link to Einstein is in the press, not in the official police text.

## Why it matters

Health data obtained without authorization is already a security incident, even when the controller says there was no publication. The tension is between the hospital note (no evidence of disclosure) and an extortion investigation, which assumes third parties hold the material.

## What to do this week

1. Treat notice to the data subject as part of the incident, not as closure.
2. Separate what the controller states from what the Federal Police confirmed: the operation does not name Einstein.
3. Record the date gap (July in the police note, 4 August in the hospital note) before using the piece in an internal report.
