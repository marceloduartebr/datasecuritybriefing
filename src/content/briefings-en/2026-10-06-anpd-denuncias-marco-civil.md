Brazil's data protection authority published its own page for reports of noncompliance with Decrees 12.975/2026 and 12.976/2026. The channel covers internet application providers, digital platforms, and suppliers of artificial intelligence systems.

ANPD does not take criminal complaints. Terrorism, inducement to suicide, and violence that requires a criminal investigation stay with the police; intimate content can also go to Ligue 180. The object of the report is the platform's duty: a notification channel, receipt confirmation, blocking of illicit ads, a representative in Brazil, a takedown deadline, a transparency report, and a ban on generating intimate content with AI.

Handling is aggregated, under articles 20, 25, and 26 of Resolution CD/ANPD 1/2021. An individual response is the exception for a serious, collective case. An anonymous report needs a verifiable element. Without the reported party's legal name, site, and email, the filing is not processed. The content may be shared with the platform's legal representative.

## What changes in practice

Platform monitoring gains a public evidence channel. An operator with users in Brazil needs an internal channel, a deadline, and a representative aligned with both decrees.
