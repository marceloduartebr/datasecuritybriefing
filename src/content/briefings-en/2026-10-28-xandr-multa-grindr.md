In one line: Ad intermediary cannot transfer responsibility for consent on special-category data.

## What happened

On October 2, 2026, Datatilsynet fined Xandr NOK 92 million (€8.6 million) for processing Grindr user data in Norway without a legal basis, from 2018 to 2020, via real-time bidding. The data indicated sexual orientation. Xandr was considered joint controller and did not verify consent.

## Why it matters

In the adtech ecosystem, the absence of evidence of valid consent at each link makes the data subject’s right ineffective. Special-category data requires technical documentation, not assumption about the previous controller.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Real-time bidding without consent verification | Datatilsynet decision | High |
| Special-category data (sexual orientation) | Grindr users | High |

## What to do this week

1. Map intermediaries that receive special-category data originated by the organization — DPO.
2. Require evidence of legal basis in adtech contracts — legal.
3. Test whether the RTB flow documents consent — security.
