**In one line:** outsourcing mail delivery also outsources custody of the recipients' data, and the incident response then depends on the operator.

## What happened

Maileva, a subsidiary of Docaposte in the La Poste group, suffered a cyberattack in mid-September 2026. The company prints and sends mail for corporate clients, such as payslips, registered letters and invoices. On November 14, La Poste confirmed to AFP that the first names, last names and postal addresses of about 22 million people had been stolen, according to Euronews. Bank details were reportedly not affected.

Maileva said it notified the CNIL and filed a complaint; a judicial investigation is under way. According to Cyberattaque.org, cited by Euronews, those affected include recipients of mail from social housing companies, from clinical laboratories of the Inovie group, and patients of the Hôpital Foch, through a subcontractor.

## Why it matters

The data subjects had no direct relationship with Maileva: the data reached the operator through hospitals, laboratories and other companies. For each of those controllers, knowing whom to notify depends on information held by the vendor. The two months between the attack and the confirmation of its scale show the cost of that dependency.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Tracking database at the operator | A third party accessed the mail tracking database | High |
| Subcontracting chain | Hospital patients affected through a subcontractor | High |
| Use of the data for phishing | Names and addresses of 22 million people | Medium |

## What to do this week

1. Map the operators that receive customer personal data, with the purpose and retention period for each — owner: data protection officer.
2. Add notice deadlines to contracts with those operators, plus delivery of the list of affected data subjects after an incident — owner: legal and third-party management.
3. Sign that it worked: when an operator has an incident, the company knows the same day which customers had data sent to it.
