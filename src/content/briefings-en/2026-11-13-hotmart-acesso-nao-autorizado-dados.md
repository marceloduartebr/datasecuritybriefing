In one line: Hotmart confirmed unauthorized access to users' personal data, detected on October 12 and notified to data subjects only on November 13; the company denies improper movement of funds.

## What happened

On November 13, 2026, Hotmart (Launch Pad Tecnologia, Serviços e Pagamentos Ltda.), a digital products platform founded in Belo Horizonte, began sending emails to users informing them that it became aware, on October 12, of unauthorized access to personal data in its systems. The notice is individualized: depending on the profile, the data involved includes name, email, payment method, transaction volume, and device information. The company states that there was no improper movement of funds, that it blocked the access of the third party involved, replaced credentials, and restricted the creation of new accesses, and that it notified ANPD within the legal deadline. In a note to Diário do Comércio, it confirmed the attack. It did not disclose the number of affected individuals, the access vector, or whether the data was copied.

## Why it matters

The 32-day interval between awareness of the incident and notification to data subjects contrasts with ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 15/2024, which provides for three business days to notify the authority and data subjects when there is relevant risk or harm. The mention of blocking a third party also points to the risk of accesses by partners and suppliers. For users, contact and transaction data make scams that impersonate Hotmart or banks more convincing, a warning the company itself issues.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure of identification, contact, and transaction data | Notice to data subjects and press note | High |
| Payment method in part of the profiles | Notice to data subjects | High |
| Improper movement of funds | Denied by the company | Low |
| Access vector and number of affected | Not disclosed | Undetermined |

## What to do this week

1. Instruct teams and customers to distrust contacts in the name of Hotmart or banks that request password, code, or payment — owner: security / communications.
2. Review third-party accesses to systems with personal data and the ability to map, per data subject, what was exposed — owner: security / privacy.
3. Monitor any statement from ANPD on the deadline for notification to data subjects.
