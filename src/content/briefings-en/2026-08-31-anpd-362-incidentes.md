In one line: the number that rises is notifications; what is missing is inventory.

## What happened

A survey released on 24 August 2026, with data from ANPD, Cetic.br/NIC.br, Sebrae and Datalege Consultoria: 362 security-incident notifications in 2025 — almost one a day and more than a quarter of the 1,508 recorded since 2021. In the same period the authority opened 81 enforcement cases and answered 12,701 data-subject requests. In a monitoring cycle with 56 agents (39 public and 17 private), only 27 fully met what was asked.

## Why it matters

A record annual volume does not, by itself, prove more attacks. Often it proves the organisation finally saw what was already happening. The most useful number in the cut is 27 of 56: a written policy does not replace the ability to list, on an authority deadline, which system holds which data subject’s data.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Incident notification | 362 in 2025; 1,508 since 2021 | High |
| Incomplete response to supervision | 27 of 56 agents in the cycle | High |
| Data-subject request | 12,701 answers in the period | Medium |

## What to do this week

1. Simulate an ANPD request for one data subject: systems, bases and access within 48 hours — owner: DPO + operations.
2. Separate what the policy claims from what the inventory can prove.
3. Signal it worked: a folder with technical evidence, not only the statement text.
