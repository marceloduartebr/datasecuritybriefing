In one line: ANPD closes the first AI sandbox without loosening the LGPD and opens the second for minors' games.

## What happened

On November 5, 2026, ANPD published the final report of the first edition of the Regulatory Sandbox on AI and data protection. The pilot followed three solutions (mobility, medical decision support, and financial) in a controlled environment, with support from CIAAM/USP. The same day the Sandbox Manual was released. The second edition was announced at the six-year balance event: focus on digital games for children and adolescents, in partnership with UNDP, aligned with ECA Digital and Decree 12.880/2026. The LGPD remained fully applicable.

## Why it matters

A sandbox without relaxation produces evidence about what the system actually reaches in the data, not just what the policy promises. For minors' games, the point is design and protection by default: notification, reward for screen time, and collection in onboarding. Anyone operating an AI feature for this audience needs an inventory of what the agent or model touches before release.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| AI feature for minors without reach map | Second edition of the sandbox focuses exactly on this | High |
| Written policy without controlled test | Manual and final report show the evidence path | Medium |
| Design that rewards screen time or excessive notification | Explicit target of the 2nd edition | High |

## What to do this week

1. Inventory AI features or games that touch minor data — owner: product + privacy.
2. Check whether there is a controlled test of what the system reaches (not just policy) — owner: security.
3. Signal: reach map updated and reviewed before any release.
