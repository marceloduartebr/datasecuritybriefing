In one line: independent DSPM keeps being absorbed by larger platforms — this time by an MDR and exposure-management provider.

## What happened

On 9 September 2026 SecureSky, of Omaha, announced the acquisition of Soveren, a DSPM vendor founded in 2021 in London that had raised US$10 million. The release does not disclose the price. According to the companies, Soveren combines at-rest data scanning with real-time network-traffic analysis via eBPF to detect leaks, usage anomalies, and changes in data flows to third parties. Claims of 98% accuracy and of being the only solution that classifies encrypted TLS traffic at scale with no performance impact come from the vendor. The technology will be integrated into SecureSky's CTEM platform.

## Why it matters

An at-rest inventory and a real-time flow answer different questions: where the data sits and where it is going, including to third parties. Anyone buying standalone DSPM also has to weigh the risk that the product changes owners.

## What to do this week

1. Confirm whether the current inventory covers data flows to third parties, not only data at rest.
2. Review continuity clauses in DSPM tool contracts — owner: security + legal.
3. Signal it worked: every personal-data flow to a third party has an owner and a recorded legal basis.
