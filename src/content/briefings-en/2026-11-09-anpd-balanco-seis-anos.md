In one line: ANPD published incident counts and a regulatory agenda that is still open.

## What happened

On November 5, 2026, Brazil's ANPD released its six-year review. The report records 1,964 security incident notices since 2021. In the first nine months of 2026 there were 423, versus 321 in the same window of 2025. Of 19 regulatory initiatives, 16 remain underway.

## Why it matters

Reporting an incident requires knowing what was reached. Without an inventory, the public number becomes the ceiling of what the organization can prove.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Incident notice | 423 in 9 months of 2026 | Medium |
| Open regulatory agenda | 16 workstreams underway | Medium |

## What to do this week

1. Check whether the last 12 months of incident records include a map of what was reached — owner: DPO / security.
2. Review whether notices to ANPD match the internal inventory — owner: legal / compliance.
3. Signal: no incident in the last 90 days without an owner and scope evidence.
