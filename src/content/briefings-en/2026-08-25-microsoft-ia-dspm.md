**In one line:** the question is no longer “will we adopt AI?”.

## What happened

The Microsoft Data Security Index, from January 2026, says 32% of data-security incidents recorded by companies in the period already involve generative-AI tools. More than 80% of surveyed organizations implement or develop DSPM strategies.

## Why it matters

Copilot and assistants generally inherit the permissions of whoever uses them — with no access policy of their own. If data access control was already loose, AI widens the gap. Adoption runs ahead of governance.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Incident involving generative AI | 32% in the Microsoft index | High |
| Inherited permission | Pattern described in the index / Copilot practice | High |

## What to do this week

1. List which assistants can reach file share, email and CRM — owner: security + data owner.
2. Treat DSPM as a prerequisite for an AI rollout, not a parallel project.
3. Signal it worked: each AI tool has an inventory of what it can read.
