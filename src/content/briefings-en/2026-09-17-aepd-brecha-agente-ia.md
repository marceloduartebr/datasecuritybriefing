In one line: AEPD reported the first personal-data breach notification in which the incident was said to have been executed by an AI agent.

## What happened

In September 2026 AEPD recorded the first personal-data breach notification in which the attack was said to have been executed by an artificial-intelligence agent. According to the affected organisation, the agent used a language model to find vulnerabilities, obtain access, alter personal data and query invoices. The agency stresses that the case is still under review and that use of a specific model does not mean compromise of the provider or of the LLM infrastructure.

## Why it matters

The point is GDPR Articles 32 and 33 in practice: a security measure for the processing and a duty to notify the authority within 72 hours when there is a risk to rights. The agent chained on its own what previously required a human operator at each step.

LATAM nexus: Spain is a RIPD peer. Since January 2026 there is reciprocal ANPD–European Commission adequacy. The precedent — an autonomous agent that authenticates, exploits and alters a record — is the kind of scenario ANPD and regional peers will demand under RIPD, LGPD Article 46 and agentic-AI governance.

## What to do this week

1. Include the AI agent in the processing inventory and in the DPIA as an access vector — owner: DPO / governance.
2. Review the 72-hour playbook to distinguish a classic incident from one orchestrated by an agent — owner: incident response.
3. Signal it worked: a clear contractual clause on who notifies (AEPD, ANPD or both).
