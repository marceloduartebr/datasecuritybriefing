In one line: The Italian Garante concluded that IQVIA's health database was not anonymous and imposed a €7 million fine.

## What happened

On 23 September 2026 the Garante adopted provvedimento n. 710 against IQVIA Solutions Italy S.r.l. Fine of 7 million euros. Database with health data on about 1 million patients from 800 family doctors, used in studies for pharmaceutical companies. Fixed code + detailed attributes allowed re-identification by reasonable means. Identifying data of more than 3,370 patients were also included. Infringements of legal basis, information, retention, DPIA and security. 120 days to remedy or anonymize via the doctors.

## Why it matters

A declared “anonymous” does not replace the re-identification test. In health data, the technical evidence of what isolates the data subject defines the real risk.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Re-identification by code + attributes | Garante provvedimento and press release | High |
| Identifying data in the same database | More than 3,370 patients | High |
| Retention without term | Data up to 2001 | Medium |

## What to do this week

1. Review databases classified as anonymized and test re-identification by reasonable means — privacy owner.
2. Check whether fixed codes or sets of attributes isolate data subjects in health data — data owner.
3. Signal that it worked: documentation of the test, not only the anonymity declaration.
