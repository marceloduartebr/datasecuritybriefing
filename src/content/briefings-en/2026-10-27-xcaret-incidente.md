In one line: Contacts of 8,300 customers left Xcaret sales systems; contact data is already a fraud vector.

## What happened

On October 25, 2026, Grupo Xcaret confirmed unauthorized access to the sales systems of its parks. Extracted emails and phones from about 8,300 customers (records from October 21), less than 0.003% of annual volume. No financial data. Individual contact to those affected and alert for fake billing SMS/emails. The company does not send payment links by text and uses only three official accounts.

## Why it matters

Contact data is enough for reservation phishing. “Unauthorized communication mechanisms” point to inventory failure of channels and accesses in sales systems. Official channel policy only works with evidence and clear communication to the customer.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Extraction of email and phone | Official company statement | Medium |
| Use for billing fraud | Company’s own alert | Medium |
| Financial data | Company states not compromised | Low |

## What to do this week

1. Inventory sales systems and channels that store or transmit customer contacts — owner: IT / sales.
2. Review official channel policy and train support to recognize and escalate fraud attempts — owner: support.
3. Confirm whether those affected received clear guidance on legitimate channels.
