**In one line:** Third pre-authentication SSRF of CVSS 10 on the same SMA1000 interface in 2026, with exploitation attempts in two days.

## What happened

On October 6, 2026, SonicWall published advisory SNWLID-2026-0017 for CVE-2026-102255, a pre-authentication SSRF (CVSS 10.0) on the Work Place interface of SMA1000 appliances (6210, 7210, 8200v). The alternative path allows a remote unauthenticated attacker to direct the device to issue requests and reach internal functionality. Affected builds include the September fixes. Fixed in 12.4.3-03670 and 12.5.0-03082. On October 8, Previdian and Defused sensors recorded exploitation attempts. Previous SSRFs on the same interface (CVE-2026-15409 and CVE-2026-83548) are in CISA KEV.

## Why it matters

The remote access entry point repeats. The build that closed the September issue reopens the October one. Edge inventory visibility and patch evidence are not the same thing.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-auth SSRF on Work Place | SonicWall advisory + decoy attempts from Oct 8 | High |
| Same flaw family in 2026 | Two prior in KEV, one with ransomware use | High |
| September builds now affected | Version list in SNWLID-2026-0017 | High |

## What to do this week

1. Inventory SMA1000 6210/7210/8200v and compare build to 12.4.3-03670 / 12.5.0-03082 — infrastructure owner.
2. Isolate or prioritize patch of those still on September builds — edge owner.
3. Confirm that SSRF attempt monitoring on the Work Place portal is active — SOC owner.
