In one line: preventive warning, not a fine: the screening AI was not yet running, and the agency demanded the data map before the pilot.

## What happened

On 23 September 2026 the AEPD published a warning (EXP202600427) to a company planning an AI tool to screen and score resumes for selection and internal mobility. The tool was not in production in Spain. The company told staff that the final decision would be human. The basis is Article 58.2.a of the GDPR: the measure does not declare an infringement; it requires privacy by design, transparency and human oversight.

## Why it matters

A human at the end of the flow does not say which fields left, how long they were retained, or whether the vendor reuses the text.

## What to do this week

1. List HR tools that read resumes — people and management owner.
2. Record fields sent, retention and training clause — privacy owner.
3. Signal: pilot only with an inventory of the data that leaves the company.
