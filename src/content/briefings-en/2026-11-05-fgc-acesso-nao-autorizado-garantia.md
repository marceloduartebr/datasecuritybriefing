**In one line:** FGC identified unauthorized access to creditor data in the guarantee payment flow; so far no evidence of leakage, misuse or financial loss.

## What happened

On November 5, 2026, the FGC updated the official statement page reporting a security incident involving personal data processed by the entity. The suspicion was identified on October 26, 2026. Affected data include name, CPF, email, date of birth, information on taxes withheld in the guarantee payment and the distribution channel of the investment in the liquidated institution. The FGC states it has not identified, to date, evidence of disclosure, misuse or financial loss. The ANPD was notified; affected data subjects as well. Access restrictions, blocking of improper accounts, evidence preservation and permission review were adopted.

## Why it matters

The FGC operates the guarantee payment to creditors of liquidated institutions. Registration and distribution channel data, in the wrong hands, increase the effectiveness of social engineering and frauds impersonating the FGC itself — a risk highlighted in the statement.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
