**In one line:** the number is threat intel; the pattern in reported incidents is still missing visibility of the data.

## What happened

Threat-intelligence reports (Intel 471, SOCRadar) indicate that Brazil accounts for up to 47% of ransomware attacks recorded in Latin America and that the region saw a 78% rise in those attacks in 2025. Finance, manufacturing, government and energy remain among the most targeted.

## Why it matters

In most incidents that become news, what was missing was not a firewall. What was missing was knowing where the sensitive data sat and who could reach it. Governance treated only as a compliance project arrives late; treated as business risk it reaches the board agenda.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Ransomware concentration in Brazil | Intel 471 / SOCRadar | High |
| Critical sectors in the crosshairs | Same reports | High |
