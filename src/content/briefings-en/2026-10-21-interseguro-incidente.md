In one line: Interseguro detected unauthorized access on 10/7 and notified customers; DireWolf lists 2.5 TB still without company confirmation.

## What happened

On October 7, 2026, Interseguro (Intercorp) detected unauthorized access to information in its systems, contained the incident and notified customers. The email informs that a third party accessed data of a group of customers, without allowing insurance operations. On October 13 the DireWolf group listed the company and claimed extraction of about 2.5 TB, including internal documents, backups and records with DNI and SOAT. The company did not confirm volume or specific categories.

## Why it matters

Gap between detection and inventory of what left. Identification and mandatory traffic insurance data cannot be replaced. Containment exists; public evidence of the scope still does not.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |

## What to do this week

1. Check for similar exposure in insurance providers or call centers — CISO.
2. Review customer data inventory and access log retention — privacy.
3. Signal: list of potentially reached records within 48h after alert.
