In one line: the number that weighs is the third party, not the employee.

## What happened

On 21 August the Qilin group claimed an attack on Cinépolis, the largest cinema chain in Latin America (headquartered in Mexico, operating in more than a dozen countries). HudsonRock infostealer intelligence points to credentials of 127 employees, information on more than 29 thousand users, 1,373 credentials of third-party staff and more than 120 points on the external attack surface. By the time of the LinkedIn briefing, Cinépolis had not commented on the scope.

A leak site and infostealer telemetry are not a company statement.

## Why it matters

1,373 third-party credentials against 127 internal ones is the typical design of box office, payments, loyalty and marketing. Exemplary internal governance does not cover the chain link.

## What to do this week

1. Inventory vendors with a login into a system that stores customer data — owner: security + procurement.
2. Demand evidence of scope (which tables, which API), not only a contract clause.
3. Signal it worked: if a third party falls today, the list of what it reaches already exists.
