In one line: A social projects reporting system at Sírio-Libanês may have exposed health and race/ethnicity data.

## What happened

On September 21, 2026 Hospital Sírio-Libanês identified possible unauthorized access to an internal reporting system tied to social projects. Data potentially involved: registration data (name, RG, CPF, address, email, phone) and sensitive data (health, race or ethnicity). No public evidence of a leak as of the October 16 disclosure. Reported preliminarily to the ANPD and the Federal Police. Limited to the social projects system; the general patient database was not affected. Individual notification is under way.

## Why it matters

Support systems and side-project systems often sit outside the main sensitive-data inventory. The response depends on knowing what that system held before it was isolated.
