In one line: ANPD moves from guidance to active enforcement of platforms on content affecting children, adolescents and women.

## What happened

On 24 October 2026 ANPD held a public hearing to review the Regulation of the Inspection Process and the Sanctioning Process. The draft incorporates competencies from ECA Digital, Decrees 12.975/26 and 12.976/26, and Law 15.352/26 that turned the Agency into a regulator. The public consultation on Brasil Participativo remained open until 26 October.

## Why it matters

The change requires technical evidence of inventory and data flows for minors and sensitive content. Written policy without a record-by-record map does not support a response to notification or sanction.
